Cinematic documentary photography, 16:9 aspect ratio. The epic and somber industrial ruins of an LPG gas storage terminal in San Juan Ixhuatepec, Mexico, 1984. Giant spherical gas storage tanks ruptured like peeled metal eggshells, scorched blackened steel beams, heavy smoke hanging over a devastated industrial lot at sunrise. Distant industrial flare stacks, emergency vehicles with flashing lights in the dusty background. Highly detailed photojournalism style, raw textures of heat-twisted metal and scorched earth, 35mm lens.
