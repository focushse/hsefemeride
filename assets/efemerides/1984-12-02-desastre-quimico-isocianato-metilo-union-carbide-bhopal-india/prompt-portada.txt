Cinematic documentary photography, 16:9 aspect ratio. The haunting abandoned ruins of the Union Carbide pesticide chemical plant in Bhopal, India. Rusted steel distillation towers, overgrown vegetation around concrete reaction vessels, crumbling industrial pipe racks against a misty dawn sky. Atmospheric documentary photography capturing historical gravity, peeling yellow industrial paint, weathered warning placards in Hindi and English, shot on 35mm film.
