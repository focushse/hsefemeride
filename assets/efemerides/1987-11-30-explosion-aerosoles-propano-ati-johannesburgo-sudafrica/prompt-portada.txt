Cinematic documentary photography, 16:9 aspect ratio. The shattered ruins of an industrial aerosol canning factory in Johannesburg South Africa, 1987. Twisted structural steel framing and corrugated wall panels blasted open, scattered metallic aerosol canisters across the concrete factory floor. Emergency fire engines pumping water into smoldering debris under an intense afternoon sun. Industrial safety investigators examining the burnt remnants of a mechanical ventilation duct and motor panel, authentic 35mm film grain.
