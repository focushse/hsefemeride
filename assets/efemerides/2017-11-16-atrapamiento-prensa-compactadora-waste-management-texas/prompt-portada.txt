Cinematic documentary photography, 16:9 aspect ratio. Interior of an industrial recycling facility in Texas, 2017. Massive horizontal hydraulic cardboard baler machine, rugged painted industrial green steel, hydraulic pistons and pressure hoses. A dark, cavernous compaction chamber with crushed cardboard bales nearby. High-visibility safety lockout padlock station mounted on an electrical control panel in the foreground with bright yellow warning signs. Dusty industrial ambient light streaming from skylights, dramatic shadows, realistic textures of scratched metal and corrugated cardboard, 35mm lens.
