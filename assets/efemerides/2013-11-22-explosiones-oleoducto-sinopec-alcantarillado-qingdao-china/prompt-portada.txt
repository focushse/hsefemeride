Cinematic documentary photography, 16:9 aspect ratio. The dramatic urban devastation scene in Qingdao China, 2013. A city avenue ripped wide open with giant slabs of shattered asphalt thrown into the air, smoke rising from deep underground storm drainage culverts. Distant Sinopec emergency crews in orange hazmat suits and fire trucks with hoses dealing with oil slicks on the torn street. Atmospheric gray coastal sky, debris, grit, authentic photojournalist reporting quality, 35mm lens.
