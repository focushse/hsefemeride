Cinematic documentary photography, 16:9 aspect ratio. The dramatic scene of the Maxima supermarket roof collapse in Riga Latvia, 2013. Twisted steel roof trusses and broken concrete slabs fallen into retail grocery aisles. Emergency spotlights illuminating the rubble in the dark rainy evening. Firefighters and rescue teams in high visibility gear working near the collapsed entrance surrounded by shattered glass and bent supermarket shelving. Authentic somber photojournalism, gritty detail, 35mm lens.
