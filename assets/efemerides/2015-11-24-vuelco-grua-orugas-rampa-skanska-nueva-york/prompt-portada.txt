Cinematic documentary photography, 16:9 aspect ratio. The dramatic aftermath of a crawler crane tip-over accident on a heavy infrastructure construction site in New York, 2015. A massive 200-ton yellow lattice-boom crawler crane fallen sideways on an inclined dirt earthen ramp, heavy steel tracks tilted in the air. The long lattice boom crumpled against concrete bridge abutments. High-visibility safety personnel, OSHA inspectors with hard hats and clipboards examining the soil and track grade. Overcast winter urban skyline in the background, 35mm lens authentic construction photography.
